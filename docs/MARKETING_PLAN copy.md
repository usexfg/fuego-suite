## 4. The Clear Economic Story

### One Sentence

> *"Fuego pays you real yield from actual economic activity — no inflation, no printing, no ponzi."*

### How It Works (the mechanism that backs the claim)

1. Hearth AMM charges a **1% swap fee** at L1 consensus level
2. Every **900 blocks (epoch boundary)** the protocol auto-harvests accumulated fees
3. **80% of fees → HEAT CD Fee Pool** (distributed pro-rata to CD holders)
4. **20% → Fuego Treasury** (self-sustaining development)
5. CDs are **HEAT-denominated** — yield is stable value, not volatile asset
6. **No governance token. No inflation schedule. No printed rewards.**

### Why This Matters

Every other yield protocol:
- Prints a farm token → pays it as "yield" → token dumps → APY was fake
- Requires governance tokens → VC unlocks → retail exit liquidity
- Depends on infinite new entrants → is a ponzi by structure

Fuego:
- Yield comes from swap fees → grows with actual usage
- No governanc token → no VC allocation → no insider advantage
- Protocol treasury is 20% cut → self-funding without inflation

## 10. Economic Alignment: Why This Story Wins

Most crypto projects tell stories that are **economically misaligned** — the founders and VCs benefit, the retail doesn't. This creates cynicism.

Fuego's story is **perfectly economically aligned**:

1. **No premine, no ICO, no VC** — nobody got tokens for free. The story is credible because the economics are credible
2. **Yield comes from fees, not inflation** — the story holds up to scrutiny because the mechanism is real
3. **80% of revenue goes to users** — the story is generous because the protocol is generous
4. **20% goes to treasury** — the story is sustainable because the protocol self-funds
5. **No governance token** — there's no "token to farm and dump." The economic loop is closed: XFG (equity) → HEAT (savings) → CD (yield) → compound or spend

**This is the most economically aligned story in crypto because it's the only one that doesn't depend on new money entering the system.**

Every comparison should hammer this point. Let the competitors defend ponzinomics. Fuego doesn't have to.
