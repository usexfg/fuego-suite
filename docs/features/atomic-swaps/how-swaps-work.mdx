## The core guarantee

An atomic swap is either:
- **Complete**: both parties receive the funds they agreed to exchange, or
- **Refunded**: both parties get their original funds back

There is no partial outcome. No escrow service holds your funds. No custodian can steal or freeze them. The exchange is enforced by cryptography.

## Adaptor signatures + Alice-locks

Fuego uses **adaptor signatures** on the XFG escrow (Musig2 joint key) plus **HTLCs on many counterparty chains**.

Current **Alice-locks** model (lab/testnet readiness varies by chain):

1. Parties exchange keys and aggregate a joint **XFG escrow** key  
2. Bob publishes adaptor secret commitment \(H(t)\) (and DLEQ as required)  
3. **Alice locks** the counterparty coin with hashlock \(H(t)\)  
4. **Bob claims** CTR with preimage \(t\) — that is the **on-chain secret reveal**  
5. Alice **extracts** \(t\); collaborative ring spend completes XFG escrow  
6. Timeouts refund each side independently  

P2P `SECRET_REVEAL` may exist as backup; **trust is the chain preimage**, not gossip.

See [SwapXFG overview](/features/swapxfg/overview) and production checklist under `docs/review/`.

## XFG-side fee

Atomic-swap protocol fees on the XFG side fund **HEAT CD yield** (and treasury share), **not** Hearth LP fees.

Exact bps/splits live in protocol config (`SWAP_FEE_CD_SHARE_PCT` / treasury share). Counterparty chains only charge their own network fees.

Hearth’s **0.3%** XFG/HEAT fee is a **separate** pipe for LPs — see [Hearth](/hearth/overview).

## Timeout and refund

If a swap stalls — counterparty goes offline, refuses to complete, or loses connectivity — the timelock expires and you reclaim your XFG via a cooperative refund transaction.

The swap daemon monitors for stuck swaps:
- Key exchange stalled for >10 minutes: warning logged
- Escrow funded but swap not completing for >30 minutes: warning logged

Neither warning triggers automatic action. You initiate the refund manually with `refund <swap_id>`.

## State machine

Swaps progress through these states:

```
ADAPTOR_KEYS_EXCHANGED (10)
    ↓
ADAPTOR_ESCROW_FUNDED (11)
    ↓
ADAPTOR_PRESIGS_READY (12)
    ↓
ADAPTOR_CTR_LOCKED (13)
    ↓
ADAPTOR_SECRET_REVEALED (14)
    ↓
ADAPTOR_XFG_SPENT (15)  ← success
    OR
ADAPTOR_REFUNDED (16)   ← refund
```

Each state transition is persisted to disk by the swap daemon, so a crash or restart does not lose your swap state.
