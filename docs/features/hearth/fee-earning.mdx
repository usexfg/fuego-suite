## Protocol flat fee

Every Hearth fill applies a **1% flat fee** (`HEARTH_FEE_BPS = 100`) on trade notional. This fee goes to the **CD yield pool**:

```
totalFee = notional × 100 / 10000   // HEARTH_FEE_BPS → CD yield pool
```

Implemented in `Blockchain.cpp` when processing Hearth/auth pool outputs.

## What LPs should expect

| Item | Reality |
|------|---------|
| Income source | **LP spread reward** = `|fill.price − P_clear| × amount` per fill — pool orders quote at a spread around P_clear, so fills away from the clearing price accrue directly to LP reserves |
| Flat fee | LPs do **not** earn the 1% flat fee — that goes to the CD yield pool |
| Share | Proportional to your LP share of the pool |
| Independence | Separate from **atomic swap** fee routing (69/11/20) |

The spread reward auto-compounds into pool reserves — realized on withdrawal via share redemption, not paid as a fee accumulator.

## What CD holders get from Hearth

The Hearth flat fee accrues toward **CD yield** plumbing (`cdHearthFeeAccumulator` → CD fee pool path). Holding HEAT CDs participates in that stream together with the CD share of **atomic swap** fees. The **LP spread reward** is LP-only; CD holders do not share it.

## Wrong numbers to ignore

Older docs and strategy notes sometimes say:

- “0.3% to LPs” as the Hearth fee — **incorrect for live `HEARTH_FEE_BPS`**
- “LPs earn the Hearth flat fee” — **incorrect**; LPs earn the swap spread, the 1% flat fee goes to CD yield
- “80% of swap fees to CDs” — **outdated**; atomic swap split is **69/11/20**

Always prefer `src/CryptoNoteConfig.h` and the consensus fee split code.
