Liquidity providers supply inventory to Hearth so traders can swap XFG↔HEAT with depth.

## What you earn

Hearth charges **1%** (`HEARTH_FEE_BPS = 100`) on trades. Consensus splits that fee:

- **50%** → CD yield accumulator  
- **50%** → LPs (pro-rata to pool share)

Plus an **LP spread reward**: pool orders quote at a spread around the clearing price (`P_clear`), and each fill earns `|fill.price − P_clear| × amount` straight into LP reserves. Both LP streams auto-compound into the pool and are realized on withdrawal via share redemption.

You do **not** earn the atomic-swap fee stream (2% = 1% + 1% per swap) unless you also hold HEAT CDs or Bonus Vault rights — that is a separate fee. Protocol earnings are exactly two streams: this 1% Hearth fee and the 2% atomic swap fee. Mint premium is 0 bps (goes nowhere); the 0.1% CD creation fee is a donation to @fuegoxfg, not protocol revenue.

## Practical notes

- Prefer dual-sided XFG + HEAT inventory when the pool design requires both sides.
- Price moves create **impermanent loss** relative to holding outside the pool; fee income may or may not offset it.
- Use wallet / daemon RPC for your build (`lp_deposit` / pool methods if exposed). Exact CLI names vary by version — check `--help` and [API overview](/api-reference/overview).

## Withdraw

Withdrawing returns your share of reserves (subject to pool state) plus accrued LP fee credit for your position. Confirm maturity / unlock rules if your position is commitment-tagged (`DEPOSIT_TERM_LP` in protocol terms).
