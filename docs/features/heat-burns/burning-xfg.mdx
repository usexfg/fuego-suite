title: "How to burn XFG"
description: "Operator outline for minting HEAT by burning XFG."
keywords: ["burn", "mint", "wallet", "HEAT"]
---

Exact wallet UI/RPC commands vary by release. Conceptually:

1. Ensure wallet is synced and funded with XFG.  
2. Invoke the burn/mint flow (wallet or RPC) specifying HEAT amount / burn amount per current UI.  
3. Confirm HEAT balance and bill-split outputs.  
4. Optionally open a HEAT CD or trade on Hearth.

See developer RPC docs for the methods exposed in your build, and [HEAT overview](/heat/overview) for economics.

<Warning>
  Burns are irreversible. Confirm amounts carefully. HEAT is a flatcoin with a purchasing-power design — not a promise of \$1 USD redemption by a bank.
</Warning>
