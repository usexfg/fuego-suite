Burning XFG is how you **mint HEAT** (HΞ∆₮), Fuego’s **colored flatcoin**.

This is **not** a third-party wrapped stablecoin. HEAT only exists on Fuego and is aimed at **purchasing power** (launch reference **\$1.58**, CPI-adj from 2009 USD — see [HEAT overview](/heat/overview)).

## What happens when you burn

1. You permanently destroy XFG (deflationary pressure; share to Eternal Flame / treasury per protocol split).
2. You receive HEAT colored outputs (bill denominations for privacy-friendly pools).
3. You can lock HEAT in **CDs**, trade **XFG/HEAT** on **Hearth**, or hold.
