## What SwapXFG is

**SwapXFG** is the product surface for **cross-chain atomic swaps** and related market UX on Fuego:

- **`xfg-swapd`** — swap daemon (initiate/accept, chain clients, peer protocol, status)
- **TUI / app** (`swapxfg/`) — operator and trader-facing UI (pairs, orderbook views, CD market surfaces, RPC)

It is **not** Hearth. Hearth is the **on-chain XFG/HEAT** exchange. SwapXFG drives **atomic** XFG↔SOL/ETH/XMR/BCH/… flows and feeds **atomic-swap fee** policy that funds **HEAT CDs**.

## Protocol shape (current)

Modern path is **adaptor-signature atomic swaps** with an **Alice-locks** HTLC model on many counterparty chains:

1. Keys + Musig2 joint escrow on XFG  
2. Bob publishes adaptor secret commitment \(H(t)\)  
3. Alice locks CTR with \(H(t)\)  
4. Bob claims CTR with preimage \(t\) (on-chain secret reveal)  
5. Alice extracts \(t\); collaborative spend of XFG escrow  

Details and readiness: production review docs under `docs/review/` (lab/testnet status).

## Supported pair labels

Daemon pairs include SOL, ETH, XMR, BCH, ARB, BASE, and related EVM clones where configured. Readiness varies by chain client (see swap production checklist).

## Fees

- **Atomic swap (XFG side):** protocol fee split — majority toward **CD yield** (buy HEAT for depositors), minority **treasury** (see config constants).
- **Hearth trades:** separate 0.3% to **LPs** — not SwapXFG atomic-swap fees.
