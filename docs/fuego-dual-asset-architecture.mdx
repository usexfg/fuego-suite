## Two assets, one chain

| Asset | Role |
|-------|------|
| **XFG** | Base privacy currency (ring signatures, decoy pools) |
| **HΞ∆₮ (HEAT)** | Colored **flatcoin** (purchasing-power unit; Fuego-only) |

HEAT is minted by **burning XFG**, held/spent as colored outputs, locked in **HEAT CDs**, and traded against XFG on **Hearth**.

## Markets and fee pipes

```text
Cross-chain atomic swaps (SwapXFG)
  → protocol XFG fees
  → mostly CD yield (buy HEAT for HEAT CD holders)
  → rest treasury

Hearth (XFG/HEAT AMM + hybrid orderbook band)
  → 0.3% swap fee
  → liquidity providers (LPs)
```

These pipes **must not be conflated** in product copy.

## Privacy stack (high level)

- **Dynamixn** ring sizes **32 / 16 / 8** (BMV 10+)
- Same-amount decoy selection
- HEAT bill denominations for shared HEAT decoy pools
- Subaddresses for receive hygiene
- Roadmap privacy (MLSAG / hidden amounts on later majors) — see privacy roadmap docs
