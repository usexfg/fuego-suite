## What Hearth is

**Hearth** is Fuego’s native exchange surface for **XFG / HΞ∆₮**.

It is **hybrid**:

1. **AMM pool** — constant-product style liquidity (seeded at HEATWAVE activation)
2. **Orderbook + market band** — pool-generated orders around a clearing price so the book has continuous depth from reserves

Trades on Hearth are **not** the same fee pipe as cross-chain atomic swaps.

## AMM pool

| Parameter | Value (code) |
|-----------|----------------|
| Pair | XFG ↔ HEAT |
| Swap fee | **0.3%** (`HEARTH_FEE_BPS = 30`) → **LP providers** |
| Genesis seed (illustrative) | ~10,000 XFG + 1,000 HEAT (10:1 seed ratio at \$1.58 CPI-adj launch) |

Providing balanced liquidity mints LP shares. LP fees accrue to LPs — **not** to HEAT CD holders.

## Hybrid orderbook & market band

Beyond pure AMM quotes, the protocol can place **pool orders** in a band around the clearing price:

- Inputs include clearing price `P_clear`, **band percent** of reserves, XFG/HEAT reserve depths, and adaptive **spread (bps)**
- `generatePoolOrders()` sizes buy/sell depth from reserves × `bandPct`
- `PoolOrderOrchestrator` decides when to **regenerate** the band (price move, reserve drift, band consumption, time)

That is the **hybrid orderbook / market-band pool**: the pool acts as a market-maker on the book while the AMM remains the deep liquidity backstop.

## Fee separation (important)

| Activity | Fee | Who earns |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| **Hearth** XFG/HEAT trade | 0.3% | **LPs** |
| **Atomic swap** (SwapXFG / cross-chain) | Protocol split of XFG-side fees | **HEAT CDs** (majority) + treasury (minority) |

Do not say “Hearth fees pay CD yield.” CD yield is driven by **atomic-swap** fee policy (buy HEAT for depositors). Hearth fees pay **liquidity providers**.
