## What HEAT is

**HΞ∆₮ (HEAT)** is a **colored flatcoin** that exists **only on Fuego**.

It is **not** a classic USD stablecoin. It is designed around **purchasing power**, not a permanent 1:1 bank-dollar peg.

| Property | HEAT |
|----------|------|
| Home chain | **Fuego only** |
| Type | Colored coin / flatcoin |
| Launch reference | **\$1.58** USD-equivalent purchasing power |
| How you get it | **Burn XFG → mint HEAT** |
| Where it trades | **Hearth** as **XFG / HΞ∆₮** |
| Yield use | **HEAT CDs** earn from **atomic-swap fee activity** (not Hearth LP fees) |

### Why \$1.58 at launch

The launch constant measures inflation on **\$1 of USD purchasing power from January 2009 through today**. Roughly: each 2009 dollar needs about **\$1.58** of today’s dollars to match the same basket of goods. HEAT’s launch reference encodes that adjustment so HEAT starts as a **purchasing-power unit**, not a naked \$1 token.

In code this is `HEAT_PEG_USD = 1.58` (`CryptoNoteConfig.h`).

### Flatcoin vs stablecoin

- **Stablecoin (common usage):** target a fiat unit (e.g. 1 token ≈ 1 USD forever).
- **Flatcoin (HEAT):** target **real purchasing power**. Launch is fixed at the CPI-adjusted reference; **quarterly inflation updates** are the intended long-run path (oracle / attestation — see design docs). Until CPI auto-update is fully live, the **launch constant \$1.58** is the on-chain reference.

Do **not** document HEAT as “USD stablecoin” or “always \$1.”

## Burn → mint

Burning XFG mints HEAT (colored outputs marked with the HEAT term). Launch mint ratio is protocol-configured (e.g. **10 XFG → 1 HEAT** seed ratio on Hearth bootstrap). Mint premiums may be zero at launch and re-enabled later.

Burn split (protocol):

- Share of burned XFG → **Eternal Flame** (permanent deflation)
- Share → **Treasury** (LP reserve / backstop — see treasury docs)

## HEAT CDs vs Hearth LPs

| | **HEAT CDs** | **Hearth LPs** |
|--|--------------|----------------|
| What you lock | HEAT (commitment deposit) | XFG + HEAT in the AMM pool |
| Yield source | **Atomic swap** epoch fees (buy HEAT → CD holders) | **Hearth** trade fees (0.3% → LPs) |
| Not funded by | Hearth LP fee share | Atomic-swap CD pool |

**Atomic swaps** (cross-chain / SwapXFG activity) fund **CD yield**.  
**Hearth** trades fund **liquidity providers**. Keep those stories separate.

## Where HEAT trades

HEAT is the quote asset on **Hearth**: the native **XFG/HΞ∆₮** market (AMM + hybrid orderbook / pool band — see [Hearth](/hearth/overview)).
