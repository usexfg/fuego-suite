Fuego is a privacy-first CryptoNote chain (ring signatures, stealth addresses) with a native financial layer: **burn XFG to mint HEAT**, lock **HEAT** in Certificates of Deposit for real yield, trade **XFG/HEAT** on **Hearth**, and move value across chains with **swapXFG** atomic swaps.

## Product path (current)

```
XFG  →  mint HEAT (burn via mintHeatV10 / Hearth TWAP)
     →  lock HEAT as a CD (term ≠ HEAT_TERM)
     →  earn yield from protocol fees (not inflation)
```

You **cannot** lock raw XFG as a CD. COLD deposits, Ember_Heat, and “XFG deposits” are **removed**. See [How CDs work](/features/commitment-deposits/how-cds-work) and [Mint HEAT](/features/heat/mint-heat).

## Core properties

**Privacy by default.** Ring signatures hide senders. Commitment deposits hide principal and interest. No one can read your CD size from the chain alone.

**Two assets, one bank.** **XFG** is base-layer private money. **HEAT** is the flatcoin (purchasing-power oriented; hearthstone reference `FCI_HEARTHSTONE_INITIAL_CENTS = 158`). CDs are always **HEAT**.

**No counterparty for CDs and swaps.** CD rules and swap atomicity are consensus / cryptography, not a custodian.

**Transferable and compoundable.** Mature or transferred CDs can roll over so principal + interest re-locks as a new HEAT CD.

## Fee structure (from live constants)

Source: `src/CryptoNoteConfig.h`.

| Fee | Rate | Routing |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Atomic swap (XFG side)** | **1%** (`SWAP_FEE_RATE_BPS = 100`) | **80%** CD yield · **20%** Bonus Vault (v11+) |
| **Hearth trade** | **1%** (`HEARTH_FEE_BPS = 100`) | **30%** CD yield · **70%** maker rebate (v11; flips to 70/30 at v12) |
| **HEAT mint** | **1%** of the burn (`HEAT_MINT_PREMIUM_BPS = 100`) | Premium XFG → AuntPoL XFG leg; the HEAT it buys → AuntPoL HEAT leg |
| **CD creation** | **0.1%** of the deposit, in XFG | Dev fund |

The atomic-swap split is versioned: blocks before v11 settled **69% CD / 11% Bonus Vault / 20% Treasury**, and reorg replay keeps that split. v11 retires the treasury leg because the treasury's Hearth LP position is now funded by the mint premium itself — the withheld premium XFG is the XFG leg and the HEAT it buys is the HEAT leg — instead of by a swap-fee cut.

These are separate mechanisms. Atomic-swap fees are not the same stream as Hearth LP fees. DIGM (if enabled elsewhere) uses its own fee constants and is **not** core Fuego onboarding.

## Dual-asset model

| Asset | Role |
|-------|------|
| **XFG** | Private base coin; burned to mint HEAT; used in Hearth and swapXFG |
| **HEAT** | Flatcoin; unit for CDs; traded against XFG on Hearth |
