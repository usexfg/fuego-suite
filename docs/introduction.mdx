Fuego is a privacy-first CryptoNote chain with **two native assets** and **two markets**:

| Asset / surface | Role |
|-----------------|------|
| **XFG** | Private base money (dynamixn rings) |
| **HΞ∆₮ (HEAT)** | Colored **flatcoin** (purchasing power; Fuego-only) |
| **Hearth** | On-chain **XFG/HΞ∆₮** AMM + hybrid orderbook band |
| **SwapXFG** | Cross-chain **atomic swaps** (XMR, ETH, SOL, BCH, …) |

## Core properties

**Privacy by default.** Dynamixn selects ring sizes **32, 16, or 8** (BMV 10+) from same-amount decoy pools. Subaddresses keep receives unlinkable to a single published address.

**No counterparty risk on swaps.** Atomic swaps are cryptographic. CDs are consensus-enforced locks.

**Purchasing-power asset.** HEAT is a **flatcoin**: launch reference **\$1.58** (USD purchasing power since Jan 2009). Intended path: adjust with inflation (quarterly) — not “print to peg \$1.”

## Fee structure (do not mix these up)

| Fee | Source | Goes to |
|-----|--------|---------|
| Atomic-swap XFG fees | SwapXFG / cross-chain | Mostly **HEAT CD yield**; minority treasury |
| Hearth 0.3% | XFG↔HEAT trades | **Liquidity providers** |
