v11 fee routing

Live constants in src/CryptoNoteConfig.h and the epoch path in Blockchain.cpp. Treasury LP only deposits when both legs match the pool ratio. A one-sided skim that cannot pair does not become liquidity.

Do not ship the full package. Reverse the Hearth split. Leave mint burns 50/50. Leave the mint premium at 0. Do not raise the CD entry fee. Fund protocol LP from balanced LP deposits, not from burns plus a 0.1% HEAT skim — those legs cannot meet.

The depositor already brought both assets at the pool ratio. A share skim cannot unbalance. This is the HEAT leg the 20% swap slice is waiting for.

Why 40 / 40 / 20 burns plus a 0.1% CD fee does not make an LP

Launch ratio: 10 XFG = 1 HEAT. Treasury LP deposits only if depositXfg / depositHeat = reserveXfg / reserveHeat. Mint 1,000 HEAT → burn 10,000 XFG. 20% of that burn = 2,000 XFG for the LP. HEAT required to pair = 2,000 / 10 = 200 HEAT. 0.1% of a 1,000 HEAT CD = 1 HEAT. To pair that one mint you need 200,000 HEAT locked as CDs. That is 200× the HEAT just minted. It will not happen. Unpaired XFG sits in m_treasuryLpPendingXfg. v11 forbids cross-conversion. The excess never enters the pool.
