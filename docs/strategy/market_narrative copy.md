# Fuego: Market Fit & Narrative Strategy

*Status: Draft*

## 1. The Core Narrative (The Wedge)

**The Market Fit:** Cypherpunk DeFi / The Private Citadel. 
Fuego sits at the intersection of privacy absolutists (who have idle capital) and DeFi yield farmers (who are tired of blockchain forensics exposing their net worth).

**The Pitch:** 
"Public DeFi is a surveillance state. Monero is money under the mattress. Fuego is the private bank that pays you to disappear."

**The Enemy:** 
Chainalysis, CBDCs, and freeze-enabled fiat stablecoins (USDC/USDT).

**The Alignment:** 
Every piece of the Fuego economy—atomic swaps, Hearth AMM, HⲶ∆T minting—feeds yield directly to Certificate of Deposit (CD) holders. Fuego economically incentivizes absolute privacy.

## 2. Context Engineering (Positioning)

Do not compete with Solana on TPS or Ethereum on TVL. Compete on sovereignty.

Position Fuego as a **financial bunker**. HⲶ∆T isn't just a stablecoin; it is algorithmic purchasing power designed to survive fiat hyperinflation entirely off-grid.

Atomic Swaps are not a "feature"—they are an **anti-delisting weapon**. Fuego cannot be de-platformed by centralized exchanges like Binance or Coinbase.

## 3. Signal Theory (Tribal Language)

Reject corporate crypto jargon. Use specific, culturally resonant terminology:
- **Epochs**
- **Hearth**
- **HⲶ∆T**
- **Fire Aliases**

Make understanding the terminology a deliberate, slight barrier to entry. This builds a cult-like adherence among early adopters (Signal Theory).

## 4. The Flywheel of Protest (Economic Action)

Frame locking a Certificate of Deposit (CD) not just as an investment, but as a **protest**. Earning yield off-grid starves the public surveillance systems of liquidity.

The 0.1% CD creation fee isn't a "take rate"—it is **"funding the resistance"** (directed to the Fuego Developer Fund).

## 5. The CLI Filter (Action Over Perfectionism)

Do not apologize for the CLI/TUI experience. Use it as a philosophical filter.

**The Story:** 
"If you want the convenience of a browser extension, accept the surveillance. If you want absolute financial sovereignty, open the terminal."
